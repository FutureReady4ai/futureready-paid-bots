Generate Activation Keys

Create a unique activation key for a customer who just paid. They'll use this key to access their paid chatbot session.

Workflow: Customer pays → you generate key → you email the key + access link → customer enters key and starts session. Keys are self-validating (HMAC-signed), no database needed.
